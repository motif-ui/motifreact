# Guide

`<Popover>` shows content attached to an anchor element, e.g. a button or an avatar. The anchor is given with the
**anchorRef** prop. The popover is rendered in the document body, so it is not cut off by the anchor's scrolling or
`overflow: hidden` containers.

There are two ways to use it:

- **Uncontrolled:** the popover manages its own state.
- **Controlled:** the state is kept in your component and given with the **open** prop. The `usePopover` hook makes
  this easier by keeping the state for you.

## Uncontrolled

When the **open** prop is not given, clicking the anchor toggles the popover, and an outside click or Escape closes it.
Only **anchorRef** is needed. **defaultOpen** may be set to show it initially.

<Canvas of={Popover.Uncontrolled} />

## Controlled

When the **open** prop is given, the popover only depends on it to appear and disappear. By default, it does not close
itself; the following props make it request a close instead:

- **closeOnOutsideClick**: a click outside the popover and its anchor
- **closeOnEscape**: the Escape key
- **closeOnScroll**: a scroll of the page or a container that moves the anchor

A close request calls **onClose** with its reason (`"outsideClick"`, `"escape"` or `"scroll"`), and the popover stays
open until the **open** prop becomes false. So the parent decides whether to close it, e.g. it may keep it open while a
form inside has unsaved changes.

<Canvas of={Popover.Controlled} />

#### With usePopover Hook

The `usePopover` hook keeps the open state of a controlled popover for you, and closes it with an outside click. It
returns:

- **open**: whether the popover is open, to be given to the **open** prop
- **toggle**: opens or closes the popover, e.g. on the anchor's click
- **ref**: to be given to the **ref** prop of the popover, so the clicks inside it are not counted as outside

<Canvas of={Popover.UsePopover} />

#### Default closing behavior

- **closeOnOutsideClick** and **closeOnEscape** are `true` when uncontrolled, `false` when controlled.
- **closeOnScroll** is `false` in both, since the popover follows the anchor while scrolling.

## Placement

**placeOn** sets where the popover is placed relative to the anchor; it is `bottom` by default. When the popover is
opened, the given placement is used if it fits into the screen. Otherwise, it is flipped to the other alignment or to
the opposite side, or shifted into the screen. The caret always points to the anchor.

While it is open, the popover follows the anchor when the page or a container is scrolled. Long text wraps within the
space at its placement instead of stretching out of the screen.

Use the **placeOn** control at the top of the page to try the placements.
